Since I really liked The Martian, I was looking forward to this one. And with the movie out, I raced through the book so I can finally let the family watch the film.

This was good. It had a lot of what we loved about The Martian - put a person in an extraordinary situation, and they have to use science to get their way out of it. There's a lot of back and forth between hopelessness and hope. And the ending surprised me - not what I expected.
