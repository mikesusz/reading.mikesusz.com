I picked this up mostly so my son (a drummer and huge blink-182 fan) could read it, but I read it also. So much of his life was abusing himself with drugs. I'm so glad he found a place where he can be clean and sober and enjoy his time with his kids. This book was published before Mark got sick, and before blink-182 got back together for the ... 4th time? But still an interesting perspective into the origins of the band, all of the other projects Travis has done, and the other businesses he's involved in.
